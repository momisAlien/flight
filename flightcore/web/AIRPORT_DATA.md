# Airport catalogue

Retrieved 2026-10-11 from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datasets/airport-codes/main/data/airport-codes.csv

Upstream: OurAirports, https://ourairports.com/data/ . Distribution and licence: https://github.com/datasets/airport-codes (PDDL; upstream public-domain data).

airports.json retains IATA code, name, municipality and ISO country for large, medium and small airports with three-character IATA codes, deduplicated by code: 8,798 records, 236 country/territory codes. Names are principally English. Selected Korean city aliases are maintained separately in simple-search.js; Korean country labels use Intl.DisplayNames.

This is a destination selection catalogue, not evidence of scheduled service, supplier coverage, availability or fares. Closed airports and heliports are excluded by airport type. Snapshot may become stale; airport codes can change.
